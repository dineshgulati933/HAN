Dinesh Gulati

Ph.D. Candidate in Water Resources

University of Idaho · Agrohydrology for Global Change Lab

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Water Resources at the University of Idaho, working with Dr. Meetpal S. Kukal in the Agrohydrology for Global Change Lab. My dissertation develops and deploys soil water budget models tailored to Idaho agroecosystems, from field-scale FAO-56 simulations and eddy covariance measurements to OpenET-based consumptive use across the Eastern Snake Plain Aquifer. I also build no-code tools such as FAO56-Studio so growers, water managers, and students can run these models themselves.

Research interests

Soil water budgeting

FAO-56 dual crop coefficient models and contributions to the open-source pyfao56 package, recipient of the Irrigation Association's 2025 Vanguard Award.

Remote sensing and consumptive use

Google Earth Engine workflows with OpenET, gridMET, and soils data to quantify consumptive water use from field to water-entity scales across the Eastern Snake Plain.

Climate-smart agriculture

How soil organic carbon gains and conservation practices change agrohydrological outcomes across soils, climates, and irrigation management.

Scientific software

Interactive web tools for agricultural water management, including FAO56-Studio and the SnakeFlux eddy covariance cooperator portal.
